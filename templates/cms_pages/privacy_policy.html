Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, place an order, make a reservation, or contact us. This includes your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store raw card data).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders and reservations, communicate with you about your orders, send promotional offers (only with your explicit consent), improve our services and website experience, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and delivering orders (e.g., delivery partners, payment processors), subject to strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, secure payment processing via Stripe, hashed password storage, and regular security audits to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, remember your cart and preferences, and analyse website traffic. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function correctly without them.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@sarabfood.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a prominent notice on our website. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@sarabfood.com or write to us at 42 Flavor Street, Manhattan, NY 10001.
