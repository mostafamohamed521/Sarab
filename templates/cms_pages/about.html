Our Story

A Passion for Great Food

Founded in 2012 by Chef Marcus and his wife Diana, Sarab started as a 20-seat diner with one simple mission: serve honest, delicious food made from the freshest ingredients. No shortcuts. No compromises.

Twelve years later, we operate across 8 cities, serve thousands of meals every week, and still hold those same values dear. Every burger is hand-pattied, every sauce is made in-house, and every customer is treated like family.
