Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Our Satisfaction Promise

If you're not 100% satisfied, we're not satisfied. We stand behind every dish we serve.

Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled within 5 minutes of placement for a full refund. After this window, cancellation may not be possible as our kitchen begins preparation immediately to ensure freshness and speed.

Food Quality Issues

If your food arrives cold, incorrect, missing items, or of unsatisfactory quality, contact us within 30 minutes of delivery with a photo of the issue. We will either re-prepare and re-deliver the order at no charge, or issue a full refund — your choice, no questions asked.

Missing Items

If items are missing from your delivery, contact us immediately via the app or phone. We will deliver the missing items as quickly as possible, or issue a partial refund for the missing items within 24 hours.

Late Delivery Compensation

If your order arrives more than 45 minutes after the estimated delivery time, you'll automatically receive a 20% discount applied to your next order as an account credit.

Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days back to your original payment method. For cash on delivery orders, refunds are applied as account credit usable on your next order.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are not available for orders where the food was delivered correctly as described, change-of-mind after the 5-minute cancellation window, incorrect delivery address provided by the customer, or orders already consumed before a quality complaint is raised.

How to Request a Refund

Contact our support team through any of these channels:

  • Email: support@sarabfood.com
  • Phone: +1 (800) 123-4567
  • Live chat on our website (9 AM – 11 PM)

Our team responds within 2 hours during business hours.
